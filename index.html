APSC 200 · Design and manufacturing

Porous impact-absorbing structure

I independently developed a porous-structure concept in CAD before working with my team to compare our designs. We selected and refined promising features to balance impact absorption, strength, low material use, mass, and manufacturability.

47.1 NStatic test load
1.858 mmSimulated deflection
0.171 MPaMaximum static stress

The team modeled, printed, and tested a 30 mm structure intended to improve protection in a goalkeeper glove. Comparing physical results with finite-element predictions revealed that the prototype was too stiff. We recommended increasing porosity to reduce mass and improve energy absorption while maintaining safe stress levels.

  • SolidWorks CAD
  • Finite-element analysis
  • 3D printing
  • Static and dynamic testing
  • Design iteration

MECH 323 · Machine design

Vehicle gearbox and shaft redesign

Our team designed a gearbox and drivetrain for a vehicle competing in speed and hill-climbing events. I completed a significant portion of the engineering calculations and helped manage team communication throughout the analysis.

We evaluated shaft reactions, forces, torque, bending moments, fatigue loading, and stress concentrations at shoulders, keyways, and diameter changes. The redesign used the DE-Gerber fatigue criterion and targeted a minimum safety factor of two with 99% reliability.

  • Shaft design
  • Fatigue analysis
  • DE-Gerber criterion
  • Stress concentrations
  • Technical communication
